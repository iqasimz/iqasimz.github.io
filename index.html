Publication

The Wildcards at Touché: Guideline-Conditioned Argument Identification with Dataset-Level Expert Routing

Muhammad Qasim Hussain, Maximilian Heinrich, Midhun Kanadan, and Benno Stein. CLEF 2026 Working Notes, CEUR Workshop Proceedings, vol. 4283, pp. 6522–6536.

We tested whether giving a model different definitions of an argument changes how well it classifies the same tweets. I presented the work at CLEF in Jena. Our submission received the Touché 2026 award for the highest Supplementary score in the argument identification task.

Master’s research

Cross-Dataset Generalization in Argument Identification: What Helps and Where Are the Limits?

A sentence can count as an argument under one dataset’s rules but not another’s. My thesis compares ways of combining classifiers, giving models the annotation guidelines, and providing the original passage or a generated record of it.
