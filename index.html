Webis group, Bauhaus-Universität Weimar

Cross-Dataset Generalization in Argument Identification: What Helps and Where Are the Limits?

  • Compared ways of combining classifiers across 17 datasets and tracked when labeled examples from a new dataset were used to tune the models or choose a rule.
  • Tested whether giving the same model different labeling guidelines helped it make the correct decisions on the same tweets, building on the published Touché work.
  • Compared original passages with generated records, then tested whether using the predicted claim role worked better than asking the model directly whether a sentence contained a claim.
  • Set up repeatable evaluations for trained classifiers and prompted language models, saving individual predictions to investigate failures as well as overall scores.
